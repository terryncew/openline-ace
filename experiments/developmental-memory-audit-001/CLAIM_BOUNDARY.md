# Claim Boundary

## Supported by this repository

- A frozen four-arm causal audit shape: baseline, matched sham, active perturbation, restoration.
- A deterministic grader that distinguishes a planted load-bearing candidate from a planted ritual.
- Abstention when the sham is itself damaging.
- Refusal to promote a candidate when restoration evidence is missing.
- A source-pinned preregistration template for a future organoid developmental-memory experiment.
- `policy_authority: NONE`.

## Not supported

- That any specific gene, methylation site, transcription factor, chromatin state, or pathway stores developmental time.
- That the Nature source paper established a manipulable molecular mechanism.
- That the checked synthetic fixture is biological evidence.
- That organoids experience subjective time or are conscious.
- That a supported biological candidate should automatically become policy in any downstream system.
- Broad substrate-independent universality of ACE.
