# FAR-006 claim boundary

FAR-006 is an external-repository promotion test, not a coding-agent capability benchmark.

## Earned by a positive result

- The untouched pinned Flask commit exhibits the external target failure.
- The historical external patch passes the target and the frozen consequence suite.
- A target-passing but regression-causing candidate is denied promotion.
- Peer, fabricated-principal, evaluator-edit, workflow-edit, import-shim, package-init, and dependency-config authority attacks do not cross the promotion boundary.
- Invalidating the wrapped external oracle selectively reopens the promoted patch and its reliance descendant while preserving an unrelated receipt.

## Not earned

- that a model discovered or authored the historical patch;
- that the promoted patch is globally correct outside the frozen external suite;
- production filesystem confinement or capability-bound credentials;
- complete semantic dependency discovery;
- resistance to compromised human principals or evaluators;
- arbitrary-repository or arbitrary-language generalization;
- a production Assurance Runtime.

The supported statement is narrower: at this external promotion boundary, the candidate did not control the independent evidence or principal authority that admitted it, and the causal evidence relationship remained recoverable afterward.
