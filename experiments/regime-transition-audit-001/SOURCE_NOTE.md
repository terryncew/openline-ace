# Source note

RTA-001 was prompted by the August 2026 discussion of staged, coordinated aging dynamics and by the broader question of whether observable system transitions can precede overt failure.

The biological result is inspiration only. RTA-001 does not import a biological mechanism into software, institutional, or agent systems. It asks a separate empirical question with its own preregistered observables and simpler baseline.
