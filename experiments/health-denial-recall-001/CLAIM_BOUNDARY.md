# Claim Boundary — HDR-001

HDR-001 is a regulatory standing-recall replay, not a clinical or legal decision system.

It may establish only that the deterministic evaluator reproduces the re-review population represented by the public corrective-action language on the frozen synthetic claim-class fixtures.

It does not establish:

- that any particular real person or claim was affected;
- the number of affected claims;
- that any affected claim should ultimately be paid;
- that any excluded claim was medically correct;
- that a Cigna coverage policy was itself medically sound;
- that the revised UM-11 cured every problem;
- that an appeal will succeed;
- that OpenLine can infer hidden dependencies from claims data;
- that the mechanism generalizes to every payer, denial type, jurisdiction, or health-benefit program.

The experiment uses no patient records and no protected health information.

The public source describes provider claims and a corrective action plan. The fixture records are synthetic abstractions of the categories named by that plan.

A result of `CAP_EXCLUDED` means only that the represented corrective action permits that category to be excluded from this re-review obligation. It MUST NOT be read as `MEDICALLY_VALID`, `PAYMENT_DENIED_CORRECTLY`, or `RETAIN` on the merits.
