# Why this move

RCDL-004 through RCDL-007 removed the broad claim that symbolic representation had unique predictive or active-search utility in the tested synthetic regime. The useful survivor was the audit discipline itself.

This experiment therefore stops asking whether ACE beats another learner. ACE becomes an instrument:

```text
candidate -> targeted break -> matched sham -> outcome oracle -> restoration -> standing
```

The strategic test is whether that experimental grammar can remain fixed while the intervention surface changes. That is a stronger and cleaner bridge than arguing from shared curves, thresholds, or vocabulary.

The control lane is deliberately simulation-only. Hardware would be a new evidence tier, not a rhetorical upgrade of this one.
