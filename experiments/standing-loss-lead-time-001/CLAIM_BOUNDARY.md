# SLD-001 Claim Boundary

SLD-001 may establish only that, in its frozen deterministic topology, dependency-bound standing invalidation can resolve LOST before a downstream headline failure while rejecting benign changes that Naive Diff invalidates.

It does not establish:

- prediction of future failure;
- completeness of a receiver-declared dependency graph;
- detection of undeclared dependencies;
- runtime correctness or safety;
- external-world lead-time advantage;
- causal discovery;
- policy or execution authority.

A hidden dependency is an explicit structural blind spot. A raw execution failure with no upstream evidence change is outside the mechanism.

`policy_authority: NONE`
`runtime_permission: NONE`
