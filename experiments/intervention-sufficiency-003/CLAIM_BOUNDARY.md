# Claim boundary

IS-003 can establish only whether one prospectively frozen, disjoint Unitree G1
simulation grid contains the preregistered amount of state-specific
intervention contrast under one released controller, one MuJoCo model, one
action set, one lag grid, and one recovery target.

It cannot establish hardware transfer, controller robustness, action
authorization, completeness of the feasible action set, a domain-independent
recoverability scalar, or superiority of any policy selector.

The 50-context IS-002 corpus is design-pilot evidence only. Its outcomes are
inadmissible for IS-003 gates, model fitting, validation, or test scoring.
Authority remains `NONE` for policy selection and execution.
