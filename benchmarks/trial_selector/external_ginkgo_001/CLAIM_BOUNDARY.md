# External Ginkgo Trial-Selector Claim Boundary

This experiment is an **external confirmation of measurement allocation**, not a therapeutic-selection or efficacy-prediction test.

The strongest allowed positive claim is:

> On the frozen independent GDPa1 complete-case cohort, the previously frozen continuous-value sequential selector found the first declared developability liability with lower assay spend than the strongest preregistered comparator, without worse false reassurance at the three-assay budget.

It does **not** establish that the selector:

- predicts clinical success, efficacy, manufacturability, or approval;
- improves the Candidate Promotion Gate result;
- may authorize promotion;
- discovers the correct developability thresholds;
- generalizes beyond this frozen external panel;
- makes missing evidence equivalent to safe evidence.

Candidates with unknown critical evidence remain unknown until measured. The selector only chooses which missing measurement to buy next.
